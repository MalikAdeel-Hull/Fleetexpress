FAQ
How is "amount owed" worked out?
It's your weekly rent minus everything you've paid during the current rental week. When it reaches £0 your status shows PAID.
My payment isn't showing — what do I do?
Payments are entered by office staff and can take a little time to appear. If it's still missing after a day, contact the office.
